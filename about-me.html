About Me

I'm a fulltime student at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia. I'm currently enrolled in the course Bachelor of Computer Science - Computer Networks and Security. I have a strong passion for cybersecurity, particularly in offensive security (red teaming), ethical hacking, and system exploitation. I also enjoy exploring computer architecture and low-level systems, as I believe understanding the hardware-software boundary is crucial for security professionals.

Born and raised in Johor Bahru, I'm a curious and driven learner who enjoys solving technical challenges and continuously expanding my skills through hands-on labs, capture-the-flag competitions, and self-learning.

My long-term goal is to become a cybersecurity architect, building secure systems and helping organizations defend against evolving threats.
